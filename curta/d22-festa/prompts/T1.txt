Light: night; the bedroom is cold blue from the monitor; the only warm light is the amber corridor light through the door. Attached: {K1} is the model sheet of HE; {K2} is the model sheet of THE FRIEND; {K3} is the model sheet of THE GIRL; {K4} is the key frame of the bedroom. Place: his bedroom at night. He sits at his desk the whole take, facing the camera over the dark back of his monitor, hands on a keyboard below the frame; the door is behind him on the right; the two friends stay in the doorway and never come in. Six shots with hard cuts. Shot 1 (close-up of HE, slow push-in, two seconds): he types fast, eyes fixed on the screen, his face lit cold blue. Nobody speaks. Cut. Shot 2 (close shot of the doorway, push-in, two and a half seconds): the door swings open and warm amber light floods in; THE FRIEND and THE GIRL lean in, dressed for a party, grinning. Only THE FRIEND's mouth moves, loud and cheerful: "Bora pra festa!" Cut. Shot 3 (close-up of HE, focus pull to his face, two and a half seconds): he glances over his shoulder without lifting his hands. Only his mouth moves, quiet: "Não vai dar. Tô trabalhando." Cut. Shot 4 (close-up of THE GIRL, push-in, two seconds): she rolls her eyes hard. Only her mouth moves, sarcastic: "Você nunca vai!" Cut. Shot 5 (close-up of THE FRIEND, tracking to include THE GIRL, three and a half seconds): he points at the desk with a mocking grin. Only his mouth moves: "E seus projetos nunca dão certo!" Only after the line has ended, both burst out laughing. Cut. Shot 6 (huge close-up of HE, slow push-in, two and a half seconds): his face falls; he swallows, lowers his eyes and turns back to the screen; behind him, out of focus, the door closes and the warm light goes out, leaving him in cold blue. Nobody speaks. The take ends in motion. Pronunciation, exactly as written: Bora is BÓ-ra; pra is one syllable, never para; Tô is one short syllable, never Estou; Você is vo-CÊ. Sound: fast typing, the door opening, the spoken lines, the two friends' laughter at the end of shot 5, the door closing; no music.
